\section{Conclusion}
RIGOR-OMR improves zero-shot recovery through staff-normalized geometry and typed relations, outperforming both baselines on Polish and SMT on Debussy. Frozen relation ablations consistently support notehead--stem reasoning and show complementary beam--stem gains, while the Debussy audit trail exposes how detections become masks, typed edges, and events. Strict OLiMPiC nevertheless shows that local correctness has not yet produced globally ordered notation; constrained binding, explicit voice modeling, and measure-consistent reconstruction are the next steps.
